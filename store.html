Surprise Furnishings Flagship Store

A Landmark in Luxury Home Décor

The Surprise Furnishings flagship store in Delhi unfolds as a 10,000 sq. ft. destination devoted to refined home furnishings and thoughtful interior expression. Curated with a discerning eye, the space presents premium upholstery, curtain fabrics, and interior solutions in an immersive setting that celebrates texture, craftsmanship, and material integrity.

Designed to foster considered design conversations, the showroom invites interior designers, architects, furniture manufacturers, and discerning homeowners to experience furnishings in real-life contexts. Rooted in understated elegance and uncompromising quality, the flagship stands as a definitive reference for timeless, design-led interiors in Delhi.
