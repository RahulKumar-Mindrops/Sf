ABOUT

Serving homeowners, corporate clients, large residences, and farmhouses, we offer customised, end-to-end solutions tailored to every space. By sourcing directly from manufacturers and partnering with world-class, design-led mills, we ensure superior quality and global standards.

Surprise Furnishings brings together thoughtful design, material excellence, and fine craftsmanship to create elevated living spaces across India. With 25+ years of experience, the brand is known for delivering interior solutions that balance comfort, functionality, and timeless appeal.
